Why we exist

The gap between the deck and the deployment.

Most companies we meet are not short on strategy. They have a roadmap, a board slide about AI, and a backlog that has not moved in two quarters. What they are short on is people who will sit inside their systems and finish something.

That is the work we take. We are deliberately small, staffed with senior engineers, and structured so the person you meet in the first call is the person in your repository in week two. When a project does not need us any more, we say so.

We work across cloud, data, applications, and increasingly AI — because in practice those are the same project seen from four sides.

How we operate

Four things we hold to.

01

Say the boring true thing

If the timeline is wrong, the data is not ready, or the idea will not pay for itself, you hear it in the meeting — not in a retro.

02

Senior people, small teams

Two engineers who know the domain beat six who are learning it on your budget.

03

Leave it documented

Runbooks, architecture notes, and a walkthrough are part of delivery. Handoff is not an upsell.

04

You own everything

Your accounts, your keys, your code, your data. Nothing depends on us continuing to exist.
