Where we push back

Three things we will not do.

Put a chatbot in front of a broken process

If the workflow underneath is unclear, AI makes the confusion faster. We fix the process definition first, and sometimes that is the whole project.

Ship without an evaluation set

A demo that impressed one room is not evidence. Every use case gets test cases and a passing bar before it goes live.

Lock you into our tooling

No proprietary runtime, no per-seat fee to us, no hostage data. If you replace us, the system keeps working.
