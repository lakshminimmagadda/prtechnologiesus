Engagement models

Three ways to work with us.

Rates and terms are quoted per engagement. Ask and we will send a number, not a range with an asterisk.

Fixed-scope project

A defined deliverable, a fixed fee, and a date. Change requests are priced before they start, not billed after.

Best when

The problem is well understood and you want a known cost.

Embedded team

A small pod — engineer, lead, and part-time architect — working on your roadmap month to month.

Best when

The work is continuous and priorities shift more than once a quarter.

Staff augmentation

Individual engineers placed into your existing team, managed by your leads under your process.

Best when

You have the plan and the management capacity, and need hands.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the situation in a few sentences. We will point at the model that fits, even when the answer is that you do not need us yet.
